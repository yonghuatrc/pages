1

Yishun MRT → Outram Park MRT

Total MRT journey: about 45–55 minutes (incl. transfer).

Step 1a — Board at Yishun:

Go to Yishun MRT Station (NS13). Take the North-South Line (Red) train southbound (towards Marina South Pier).

Step 1b — Ride & transfer:

Stay on the train for 10 stops and alight at Dhoby Ghaut (NS24).

At Dhoby Ghaut, follow the signs to the North-East Line (Purple) platform. Take the train southbound (towards Harbourfront) for 2 stops.

Step 1c — Alight at Outram Park:

Get off at Outram Park MRT (NE3 / EW16). Follow signs to Exit 7 (NOT Exit 6 — that one is closed).

Stops breakdown (NS line, southbound):
Yishun (NS13) → Khatib (NS14) → Yio Chu Kang (NS15) → Ang Mo Kio (NS16) → Bishan (NS17) → Braddell (NS18) → Toa Payoh (NS19) → Novena (NS20) → Newton (NS21) → Orchard (NS22) → Somerset (NS23) → Dhoby Ghaut (NS24) — alight here.

Then transfer to NE line (follow signs at Dhoby Ghaut): Dhoby Ghaut (NE6) → Outram Park (NE3) (2 stops).

2

Exit 7 → Walk to SGH Block 6

After tapping out at the gantry, look for the sign that says "Exit 7" and head up. The sheltered walkway will lead you across to SGH.

Walking directions (about 16 min, 1.1 km):

  • Come out of Exit 7 and take the sheltered walkway heading west.
  • Follow signs saying "SGH" / "Singapore General Hospital" (blue hospital signs).
  • Walk along the overhead bridge / linkway — you will pass the Health Promotion Board building on one side.
  • Come down the linkway into SGH campus. You will see the main hospital blocks ahead.
  • Follow road signs pointing to "Block 6". Block 6 is on the western side of the campus, near Hospital Crescent.
  • Look for the Block 6 / Block 7 lift lobby — there is a covered drop-off area in front.
Don't see Block 6? Ask any SGH staff at the road junction, or call 6222 3322. The campus has multiple blocks (3, 4, 5, 6, 7) connected by sheltered walkways. Block 6 is connected to Block 7 — the lift is shared and is the key access point.

3

Inside SGH Block 6 → Ward 63, Room 22, Bed 3

Honest disclaimer: SGH wayfinding signs change, and inside-Block-6 layout (especially which floor Ward 63 sits on) can vary. The instructions below are based on SGH's official campus map. If anything doesn't match, ask at the Block 6/7 lift lobby or the SGH information counter on Level 1 — they will direct you.

Once you're at the Block 6/7 lift lobby:

  • Enter Block 6 / Block 7 lift lobby (the entrance from Hospital Crescent, ground level).
  • Look for lifts signposted "Wards 63, 64, 68" — these are visitor lifts serving the upper floors of Block 6.
  • Take the lift up. (Per SGH campus map, Ward 63A is on Level 3. For a general ward like Ward 63, the floor is likely 3 as well, but the lift panel will tell you — press the button for the level marked "Ward 63".)
  • When you exit the lift, follow the corridor signs to "Ward 63".
  • At the Ward 63 entrance, there will be a nurse station / counter. Tell them: "I'm here to visit [friend's name], Room 22, Bed 3."
  • The nurse will confirm the bed and let you in (SGH has visiting hours and visitor registration — bring NRIC/passport).
  • Look for the room number on the door — find Room 22 and look for Bed 3 inside.

Block 6/7 lift lobby to Ward 63 (best-effort schematic).

Tip: "Room 21/22" in the same ward is common at SGH. The nurse station will know exactly which room. Don't wander — always ask the nurse station first so you don't accidentally enter the wrong room.
Visiting hours reminder: Most SGH wards allow visiting 12pm–2pm and 5pm–8.30pm daily, but these can change. Check SGH's website or call 6222 3322 to confirm before you leave home. Ward 63 may have additional restrictions if it's an isolation or high-dependency ward.
4

Returning home to Yishun

Reverse the route:

  • From Block 6, walk back via Hospital Crescent to Outram Park MRT Exit 7.
  • Tap in at Outram Park, take NE Line (Purple) northbound (towards Punggol) for 2 stops to Dhoby Ghaut (NE6).
  • Transfer to NS Line (Red) northbound (towards Jurong East) for 10 stops back to Yishun (NS13).
